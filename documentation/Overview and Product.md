A small, wearable name-tag type device that one can use when "in the field" to quickly capture notes by pressing a button and talking. At the end of the day, the user will be able to receive a timestamped recap about everything that happened throughout the day. Help organize what happened and highlight any urgent things they need to taken care of. 

## Product ideas
Why a nametag?
- easy to clip on and off
- close to mouth so dont have to talk into wrist for example
- small and easy to give to others, we want a minimal change in workflow
We also discussed modular hardware. maybe the device can also be attached to a clip that be clipped onto a hat.

- charge per device? buy 3 devices for $99 and have a free version, pay extra subscription for the AI pro features. somethign like an extra $10 a month.
